# Competitor observations

Research date: 26 October 2026. Scope: 29 unique domains under the fashion heading of the supplied competitor sheet. Trendtrack data is directional research, not verified merchant analytics.

## Volume signals

The highest reported monthly traffic in this set was Harper & Wells (1,695,334), Drune (1,540,290), Nordheim Mode (896,259), Bristey (392,588), Leon Boutique (343,478), Lozenda Fashion (314,839) and Karlson Berlin (301,108). The reported traffic month was September 2026. Bristey's current site sells homeware and was excluded as a fashion design reference.

Among stores with reported sales estimates, Renley had the highest last-30-day estimate ($373,135), followed by Brooklyn Ridge ($210,068), Oliva Mode ($168,005) and Theodore Townsend ($114,581). Many other stores had no sales estimate. These figures do not establish a complete revenue ranking.

Traffic and sales are separate estimates with different windows. For example, Renley's traffic estimate and sales estimate differ substantially in scale. No conversion rate or average order value was inferred by combining them. Ad counts measure detected activity, not profitability; a zero does not establish that a store is not advertising.

## Observations translated into the theme

| Reference | Observed pattern | Theme implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Harper & Wells](https://harperwells-boston.com/) | Separate men's and women's bestseller routes; service information and FAQ | Editable women's/men's collection sections, native collection grid and optional service blocks |
| [Harper & Wells product page](https://harperwells-boston.com/collections/men/products/mason-shirt) | Colour and size selection near the CTA; product benefits and delivery/returns information | Native variant picker and buy buttons retained; optional product information blocks close to the CTA |
| [Drune](https://drune.de/) | Simple women's/men's category entry points and visible contact/policy routes | Two-card editorial entry, native menus and footer links |
| [Nordheim Mode](https://nordheim-mode.de/) | Bestseller collections, category routes, service messages and quantity-discount promotion | Native featured collections and configurable service messages; no automatic discounts without store configuration |
| [Renley](https://renleyco.com/) | Visible colour options, bestseller merchandising and price comparison | Native Dawn swatches, quick add, image switching and Shopify price rendering |
| [Brooklyn Ridge](https://brooklynridge.com/) | Named collections, editorial collection stories and brand reassurance | Editable image-and-text story, collection cards, brand footer and three design presets |

These are design hypotheses. The available data cannot prove which design elements drive revenue. No competitor brand assets, photographs, reviews or policies were copied.

## Decisions for a 45+ audience

Readable body text, larger option controls, visible focus, descriptive labels, clear category routes, no entrance animations by default, product imagery before long sales copy, and service details that can be checked against actual policies. Native product descriptions and app blocks support more detailed fit and sizing information.

## Measurement after launch

Use Shopify analytics and the store's consent configuration to review mobile product-page engagement, add-to-cart rate, checkout completion, returns related to sizing, and revenue per session. Review UK and US separately. Benchmark real mobile Core Web Vitals after products, photography and apps are installed. Do not attribute any observed lift to the theme without a suitable comparison.
